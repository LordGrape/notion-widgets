A careful transition

Preserve the record. Reset the assumptions.

Original saved data is copied to a private recovery record. Existing ratings are not converted into verified learning evidence. Imported questions remain drafts until you confirm their sources and practice permissions.

Evidence & limitations

A practice engine. Not a legal oracle.

Try first. Check second.

Your original response and self-reported conditions are recorded before the answer checklist appears. Full sources stay in Notion.

Keep the source above the model.

Assigned sources, current professor guidance, and official rubrics outrank generated material. External AI feedback is always provisional. No AI API is called by this version.

Make narrow, attributed observations.

Pattern summaries require at least three source-checked, closed-note, unaided attempts on two days. This is a conservative product rule, not a validated mastery threshold. Repeating one question does not establish transfer.

Schedule without false precision.

Review intervals and time estimates are editable starting points. Completion, speed, and self-assessment do not become a mastery percentage or predicted exam grade.

Protect practice history.

Responses and assessments are append-only. Corrections create a new assessment without replacing the old one. Imports are checked before confirmation; backups stay private.
