# Evidence basis and validation boundaries

Research informs the practice design. It does not validate this app, its legal assessments, its intervals, or personalized optimality.

## Starting points

Retrieval practice has shown benefits in applied classroom research. Implementation, materials, feedback, and the comparison activity matter; quizzing is not universally optimal. [^https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-021-09595-9]

Law-school-specific observational work links application-oriented study and self-testing with academic performance. These associations support testing a practice loop, not claiming this app will cause better grades for a Canadian law student. [^https://www.slu.edu/law/law-journal/-pdf/issues-archive/v62-no2/jennifer_cooper_and_regan_gurung_article.pdf]

Georgetown's examination guidance emphasizes practice and examination-answer analysis. This is expert guidance, not a controlled evaluation of Study Engine or a substitute for current professor instructions. [^https://www.law.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Updated-FINAL.Exam_Tips_Handout.pdf]

These sources justify adding demonstrated performance to reading and note-taking. They do not justify treating generated feedback as legal authority.

## Product choices, not empirical findings

| Choice | Actual status |
| --- | --- |
| Six practice categories | A task taxonomy, not a validated scientific ladder |
| 20-minute starting budget | An adjustable workload choice |
| 1 / 3 / 7-day defaults | Transparent heuristics, not fitted forgetting estimates |
| Three eligible attempts on two days | A conservative evidence-display safeguard |
| Source-checked self-assessment | An attributed judgment, not independent grading |
| Repeated questions | Evidence about those responses, not proof of transfer |
| Response duration | Wall time that may include interruptions, not a speed diagnosis |

## Source and AI discipline

Read the actual assigned source before drafting a legal question or answer checklist. Require an existing link and exact support. Incomplete student notes and AI citations are not automatically authoritative.

This version calls no AI model. Notion AI may prepare upstream packs; every imported checklist begins unverified. External AI feedback stays provisional and is excluded from independent-performance summaries.

Instructor feedback is labelled as feedback the user recorded and requires a link. Authorship and unaided conditions are not independently authenticated.

No Canadian-law grading benchmark or inter-rater agreement has been established here. Correlation in another grading study would not establish per-answer accuracy in this product.

## Pilot

Use one permitted course source set. Record an initial unaided response, revisit after a delay, and include a genuinely new question. Compare with assigned material and actual instructor feedback when available.

Review actual time, verification burden, repeated errors, and whether the routine is used. Adjust from those observations. Do not infer statistical improvement from a few samples, or claim continuous monitoring without data.
